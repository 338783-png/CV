CV template for French-speaking Africa

In Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Togo, Benin or Burkina Faso, a CV is not laid out quite as it is in Europe. Here are the most common customs, and a template that follows them.

What you will find most often

  • A full “Personal details” block: full name, date and place of birth, nationality, marital status, address, phone, e-mail.
  • A recent passport-style photo, on a plain background, in appropriate dress.
  • Education in detail: the exact qualification (baccalauréat and its series, BTS, Licence, Master), the institution, the year. For a recent graduate, it comes before work experience.
  • Internships and work experience, with dates, the company and two or three concrete tasks.
  • Languages, including national languages: speaking Wolof, Dioula, Ewe or Lingala is an asset for many field positions.
  • References: one or two people (a former manager, a teacher) the recruiter can call. Let them know beforehand.
  • A declaration of accuracy at the bottom of the page: “I hereby certify that the above information is true and correct”.

Mistakes that cost you an interview

  • A phone number without the country code, or one that is no longer in service. Give a number where you can be reached, and say whether it is on WhatsApp.
  • A fanciful e-mail address. Create one with your name.
  • Three pages or more. One page is enough at the start of a career, two at most later on.
  • Spelling mistakes. Ask someone to proofread your CV.
  • A Word file whose layout breaks from one phone to another. Always send a PDF.

What about an NGO or an international company?

International organisations and many large companies prefer a shorter CV, focused on skills and results, often without marital status. Read the job advert: if a format is requested, follow it. Otherwise, the classic template or the European template is a better fit than the template with full personal details.

Names are spelled correctly

Letters specific to African languages (ɛ, ɔ, ŋ, ɓ, ɗ, ẹ, ọ…) are supported: your name appears on the PDF exactly as you write it.

The “African” template includes all of these elements. You fill in the form, it does the layout.
