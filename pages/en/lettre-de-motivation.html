Cover letter: the template that works

A good letter fits on one page and answers a single question: why you, for this job, in this company? Here is the expected layout and the plan to follow.

The layout

  • Top left: your name, address, phone and e-mail.
  • On the right: the recipient's name or position, the company, its address.
  • Place and date: “Dakar, 14 November 2026”.
  • Subject: “Application for the position of…”, with the advert's reference if there is one.
  • Greeting: “Dear Sir or Madam,”, or the person's name if you know it.

The three-paragraph plan

  1. You: why this company and this position interest you. Show that you have done your research.
  2. Me: what you can do, with one or two precise examples from your education or your experience.
  3. Us: what you can bring, and your availability for an interview.

Example outline

Dear Sir or Madam,

Currently [your situation], I am writing to apply for the position of [position] at [company], whose [what attracts you: activity, project, reputation].

During [education or experience], I [concrete achievement, with a figure if possible]. This experience allowed me to develop [skill useful for the position].

I would be glad to put these skills at the service of your team, and I am available for an interview at your convenience.

Yours faithfully,

Rules to follow

  • One page, no more. Short sentences.
  • One letter per advert: do not send the same one to everybody.
  • Do not copy your CV: choose two examples and develop them.
  • No mistakes. Have it proofread.
  • For a job application handed in on site, some administrations ask for a handwritten letter: in that case, use the PDF as a draft to copy out.
